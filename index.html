Measured, not promised

30 seconds from a fresh load: 30 kills, 32 shots, 96% accuracy, and the ultimate fired itself twice. That is one kill per second on a normal arena.

Three steps

No key, no checkpoint, no ad link.

  1. Join the game and get into an arena round, not the lobby.
  2. Open your executor and paste the line.
  3. Press START FARM on the panel. It remembers your settings next time.

If nothing shows up

Run it while a round is live. In the lobby the script tells you and stops instead of throwing a wall of red text.

Any executor with loadstring and game:HttpGet works. gethui, readfile, writefile and hookfunction are optional — without them it still runs, it just forgets where you dragged the panel.

What the panel does

Every control is a button. Drag it anywhere, mouse or finger.

  • START FARM / STOP — the whole loop.
  • BACK — how far behind the target you stand. 0 works, so does 8.
  • BOTS / PLAYERS — pick what counts as a target.
  • RESPAWN — deploys you again the moment you die.
  • INF ONESHOT — fires the ultimate the moment it is charged, forever.
  • HOP SERVER — jumps to the fullest server it can find. More targets, more kills.

Live readout

Round state, current target and its health, ammo, accuracy, ultimate charge, how many players and bots are in the arena, and shots / landed / kills / reloads.

Updates

The link never changes. Paste it once, you always get the newest build.
