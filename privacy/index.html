NEWGOD Convert — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

NEWGOD Convert does not collect, transmit, store or sell any data about you. It has no server. It makes no network requests. Your files never leave your computer.

What the extension does with your files

When you drop a file into NEWGOD Convert, the file is read by your own browser into your own computer's memory. The conversion runs there. The result is handed back to you as a download through a blob URL created by your browser.

At no point is the file, its name, its contents, or any information about it sent anywhere. There is no upload step, because there is no place to upload to.

What the extension stores

Two things, both in your own browser's local storage, on your own machine:

  • The pixel animation you draw in the ANIMATION tab
  • Your editor settings, such as board size, frames per second and export size

This data never leaves your browser. It is not synced, not backed up to any server, and not readable by us or by anyone else. Clearing your browser data removes it.

If you load your own GIF, MP4 or WebM as the loading animation, that file is held in memory for the current session only and is not saved anywhere.

Permissions

The extension's manifest declares:

  • no permissions
  • no host_permissions
  • no content_scripts

It therefore cannot read the pages you visit, cannot see your browsing history, cannot access your cookies, and cannot make requests on your behalf.

Network activity

There is none. A search of the entire source for eval(, new Function, fetch(, XMLHttpRequest, http:// and https:// returns zero matches for all six. There are no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting and no third party libraries loaded at runtime.

Data we collect

None. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • personally identifiable information
  • health information
  • financial or payment information
  • authentication information
  • personal communications
  • location
  • web history
  • user activity

Data we sell or share

None, because we hold none. We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties. We do not use or transfer user data for any purpose unrelated to the item's single purpose. We do not use or transfer user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

Children

The extension collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change with it. Because the extension collects nothing, any future change would have to be announced through a new version of the extension and its store listing.
